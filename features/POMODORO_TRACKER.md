# Feature: Pomodoro Focus Timer & Productivity Analytics

Structured time management technique with session cycle dots and focus statistics.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Cycle State Machine**: Focus (25m) -> Short Break (5m) -> Long Break (15m after 4 cycles).
2. **Session Dots**: Tracks completed cycles visually with glowing indicators.
3. **Analytics Dashboard**: Automatically increments total focus minutes and completed sessions in `localStorage`.
