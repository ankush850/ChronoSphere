# Feature: Smart Multi-Alarm Scheduler

Schedule daily alarms with custom labels, melody selection, and ringing overlays.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Schedule Checker**: Evaluates enabled alarms against current `HH:MM` time every second.
2. **Alert Overlay**: Displays fullscreen ringing backdrop with bell animation and alarm title.
3. **Snooze & Dismiss**: 5-minute snooze automatically creates a temporary alarm schedule.
